Published list prices

One flat number, set by the budget you approve.

The monthly fee is a flat number set by the ad budget you approve, never a percentage of it. You pay Meta directly from your own account, and ad dollars never appear on an invoice from us.

Nothing is due when you sign. The setup fee falls due in two halves: half when the written setup plan is in hand at the end of week one, half when the first approved campaign is live.

Every band includes the same rules: every change to your ad account is paused until a named person approves it, every approval is written to a record you can read, your written budget cap and its pause-only circuit breaker are in force, and the numbers page opens from a link, no account required. Your band changes only when you approve a budget that moves it, and the change takes effect the following month, up or down. Under about $1,000 a month in ad spend we'll usually tell you to keep it in-house. At $50,000 a month and above, the proposal quotes one flat number.

02Never charged

Three things we never charge.

  • A percentage of ad spendThe monthly fee is the flat number for your band, never a share of what you spend.
  • A markup on mediaYou pay Meta directly from your own account. Ad dollars never appear on an invoice from us.
  • A fee per resultNothing we charge is tied to a lead, a call, a booking, or a sale.
03Terms

Three months, then month to month.

The monthly fee starts the month something is live. It runs for three months, then month to month on thirty days' notice.

If you leave, you keep the account, the pixel, the creative, and the record.
