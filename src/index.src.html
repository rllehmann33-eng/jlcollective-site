01 The rule

The same rule governs an ad account and a dining room.

Every change waits for a name.

Everything we run shares one rule: nothing changes until a named person has approved it. In a client’s ad account, that means every budget move, every paused ad and every new campaign is held until one of our principals or one of the client’s own people signs off by name. In a restaurant, it means no gesture reaches a guest until a manager says yes.

Every approval is written to a record that can only be added to. Nothing in it can be edited or removed afterward, by the client or by us. If anyone asks who changed what, and why, and when, the answer is already there.

The software we build exists to keep that rule, not to replace the person. It watches, drafts and files the paperwork. The one exception is the circuit breaker a client authorizes in advance, and all it can do is pause spend.

What the record refuses, and what the spending circuit breaker cannot do, are set out on the trust page.
