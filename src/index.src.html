01The rule

Every change waits for a name.

We run paid media on one rule: nothing reaches your ad account until a named person has approved it. A budget change, an ad paused or revived, a new campaign, a fresh piece of creative — each one is drafted with its reason attached and held, paused, until one of our principals or one of your people signs off on it by name.

Every approval is written to a record you can read at any time. Nothing in it can be edited or removed afterward, by you or by us. If you ever ask who changed what, and why, and when, the answer is already there.

Behind the approvals sits a budget cap you set in writing, and behind the cap a circuit breaker you authorize in advance, by name. It can do exactly one thing: pause spend. It can't raise a budget, launch anything, or move money. Our software watches the account and drafts the paperwork; it has no other way to touch your account. The calls are made by people.

03How it works

A free audit, then a priced engagement.

  1. 1The audit — free

    Email us and grant read access. We'll write up what your spend is actually doing and what we'd test first. The write-up is yours to keep either way.

  2. 2The proposal — on paper

    From the audit we propose the shape — what we'd run, what needs setting up, and what it costs. The monthly fee is a flat number set by the ad budget you approve, from the published bands; the proposal says which band your account is in, why, and what the one-time setup will be. Every number is on paper before you owe anything.

  3. 3The engagement — a name on every change

    We run the account. Every change is paused until a named person — one of ours, or one of yours — approves it, every approval goes on the record you can read, and your budget cap and its pause-only circuit breaker are in force from the first day.

If the audit says your ads are fine, we'll say so, and that's the end of it. The write-up is yours to keep, even if you use it to run things without us.

06Questions

Cost, control, and what happens to your data.

Is software changing my account on its own?

No. Our software watches the account and drafts proposals; a named person approves each one before it reaches your account, and that approval is kept on a record you can read. The one exception is the circuit breaker you authorize in advance, and all it can do is pause spend. Nothing ships without a name attached.

What does it cost?

The audit is free, and the write-up is yours whether or not we go further. If we do, the monthly fee is a flat number set by the ad budget you approve, never a percentage of it, from $900 a month at the smallest band. The bands, the one-time setup, and the three things we never charge are published in full on the fee schedule, and the proposal repeats them for your account before you owe anything.

We already have an agency.

Keep them. The audit doesn't ask you to fire anyone — run it, read it, and decide with numbers instead of a pitch.

What industries do you take?

Jack goes deepest in automotive. We take work where the outcome can be measured and a named person can stand behind every change.

Who actually does the work?

The people on this page, with a named principal on every account. Software monitors and drafts; the principal decides what ships.

What happens to our data?

Your ad accounts stay yours — we work inside them with access you grant and can revoke whenever you want. We keep the records our work produces (audits, reports, the log of approved changes) so you have a history, and we hold the access you grant until you revoke it. We don't sell data, move it between clients, or feed it into model training; our software sends account data to the AI models it calls only to do your work. Details are in the privacy policy and on the trust page.
