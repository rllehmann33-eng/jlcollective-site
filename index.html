01

What we do

We run the marketing and build the software under it.

The work is paid media, primarily Meta. We take the accounts over, plan the campaigns, run them week to week, and tell you what happened. There is no account manager relaying messages, because there are only two of us and we are the ones doing it.

We wrote the software the work runs on. Once we are in the account it watches continuously, prepares the changes it thinks should happen, and waits. Nothing spends until one of us approves it. Reports come out of the log of what actually ran, so what you read in the report is what happened in the account.

Custom software

Some businesses need a system that does not exist yet: an internal dashboard that finally shows the right number, a quoting tool, a booking platform. We design it, build it, and stay on it after launch. We would rather own something for years than hand it over and leave.

05

Questions

Who actually works on our account?

Luke, day to day, with Jack on the relationship. That is the whole firm. Write to hello@thejlcollective.com and it reaches both of us.

What does it cost?

It depends on how much you spend and how much work the account takes. We price a monthly fee against something tied to performance. The audit is where we both find out whether the numbers make sense, and it costs you nothing.

Do you work with our competitors?

We take one client per market. If that is a conflict when you write to us, we will tell you on the first call.

How quickly do things actually happen?

Once we are in the account, the monitoring and the preparation run continuously. The only step that waits on a person is the decision to spend, and that decision is one of ours. A change can go live the same day you ask for it.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your advertising accounts, which you own. We work inside them with access you grant and can revoke whenever you want. We do not sell data, share it between clients, or feed it into model training. Details are in the privacy policy.

We need something custom built. Is that you?

Probably. Applications, marketplaces, quoting and booking systems, internal tools. Tell us what the system needs to do and we will tell you plainly whether we are the right people to build it.
