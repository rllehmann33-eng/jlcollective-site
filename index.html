How we run your account

The work, and the limits on it.

We read your account every day. When something should change, we draft the change with its reason attached: a budget step, an ad paused, a new campaign created paused. A named person, one of our principals or one of your people, approves or declines it before it reaches the account. You read your numbers from a private link, no login, and get a weekly report with every change and who approved it.

Behind your written budget cap sits a circuit breaker you authorize in advance. It can only pause spend. It can’t raise a budget, launch anything, or move money.

Every decision is written once, with a name on it. Nothing in the record can be edited afterward, by you or by us. When you leave, you can take a copy, and it is deleted with the rest of your data within 30 days.

The fee bands, the record and the three real screens are on the paid media page. What the record refuses, and what the breaker cannot do, are set out on the trust page.
