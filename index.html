01

What we built

01 / 06

Most agencies sell hours. We built software.

The engine connects to your ad accounts, your reviews, and your numbers. It flags waste, drafts campaigns, catches what's working while it's still working, and turns the Monday spreadsheet ritual into a report that writes itself.

We built the operating system — the planning, the guardrails, the approvals, the record. The models it calls are tools inside it, not the product.

It doesn't get bored and it doesn't miss a week. What it never does is act alone: every change ships with a human's name on it. That's why the work holds up.

03

How it works

03 / 06

Free audit, free test, then a priced engagement.

1

The audit — free

Email us and grant read access. We'll write up what your spend is actually doing and what we'd test first. The write-up is yours to keep either way.

2

The two-week test — free

Two ways to run it. Watch mode: the engine shadows your account, and at the end you get the report — what it would have changed and what that was worth. Live mode: the engine proposes changes, someone on your team approves each one, and you keep whatever the two weeks win.

3

The engagement — priced after the test

We propose the shape — what we'd run, what we'd build, and what it would cost — after the test, when we're both looking at the same numbers.

Our promise
If the audit says your ads are fine, we'll say so, and that's the end of it.

The audit costs nothing and the write-up is yours to keep — even if you use it to run things without us.

05

FAQ

05 / 06

Cost, control, and what happens to your data.

So AI is running my account?

The engine drafts and flags. A person approves — and during a live test, that person is on your team. Nothing ships without a name attached.

What does it cost?

The audit and the two-week test are free. The engagement is priced after the test, when we both know what the work is. Every number is on paper before you owe it.

We already have an agency.

Keep them. The audit doesn't ask you to fire anyone — run it, read it, and decide with numbers instead of a pitch.

What industries do you take?

Jack goes deepest in automotive. The engine doesn't care what you sell — if the outcome can be measured, we'll take it on.

Who actually does the work?

The people on this page, with a named principal on every account. The engine monitors and drafts; the principal decides what ships.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your advertising accounts, which you own. We work inside them with access you grant and can revoke whenever you want. We don't sell data, move it between clients, or feed it into model training. Details are in the privacy policy.
